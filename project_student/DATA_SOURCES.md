# Data sources

All original data lives under `data/raw/`.

## Income

- **File:** `data/raw/ons_income_msoa_2023.xlsx`
- **Source:** ONS, *Income estimates for small areas, England and Wales — financial year ending 2023*. https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/personalandhouseholdfinances/incomeandwealth/bulletins/smallareamodelbasedincomeestimates/financialyearending2023

## MSOA boundaries

- **File:** `data/raw/MSOA_2021_London.geojson`
- **Source:** ONS Open Geography Portal, *MSOA (December 2021) Boundaries EW BGC V3*. https://geoportal.statistics.gov.uk/datasets/middle-layer-super-output-areas-december-2021-boundaries-ew-bgc-v3-2/about

## Census 2021

- **Folder:** `data/raw/census/`
- **Source:** Nomis bulk Census 2021 downloads. https://www.nomisweb.co.uk/sources/census_2021_bulk

## Want something else?

- Other MSOA-level ONS datasets (house prices, deprivation, etc.) live at https://www.ons.gov.uk and https://www.nomisweb.co.uk.
